
*Weapon, Uncommon*

The sword's blade is pitted with rust-like decay, yet the steel beneath gleams with a strangely lively temper. A faint greenish sheen plays along the edge, and a persistent, sickly-sweet odor clings to the metal, as if the blade itself is slowly rotting.

**Putrid Smite.** When you hit a creature with this longsword, the blade automatically unleashes a mini-smite of rot. The target takes an extra **1d4 necrotic damage**. If the target is a living creature, it must succeed on a **DC 13 Constitution save** or have its speed reduced by **10 feet** until the start of your next turn as decay grips its limbs.

**Lore.** Alcance 5 pies. Esta espada Lanza automáticamente un mini-smite sobre la criatura. (Apesta bastante) _Golpe:_ 7 (1d8 + 3) daño cortante + 2 (1d4) de daño necrótico (Mini-smite de putrefacción).
